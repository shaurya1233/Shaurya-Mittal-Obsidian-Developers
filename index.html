    II · Chapter the first

    The Author

    Shaurya Mittal — Student Developer, AI-Assisted Digital Creator, Lucknow, India.

    Shaurya Mittal is an independent student developer and AI-assisted digital creator from Lucknow, India.

    His primary interests include building websites, designing digital interfaces, experimenting with browser technologies and exploring the possibilities of artificial intelligence in software development.

    He works on independent digital projects ranging from premium business websites and interactive browser applications to creative experiments and digital experiences. His approach combines independent learning, experimentation, AI-assisted workflows and hands-on project development.

    He aims to develop increasingly sophisticated digital products and establish himself through the quality and functionality of his work.

                    X · The colophon

                    Colophon

                    NOUMEN — the digital home of Shaurya Mittal’s development projects, experiments and creative work.

                    NOUMEN represents an approach to independent digital creation, where design, development, experimentation and deployment come together.

                    It showcases website projects, interactive interfaces, browser-based experiments, creative technology and digital experiences.

                    Created by Shaurya Mittal as a single HTML file. Set in Cormorant Garamond and Instrument Sans, with system serif fallbacks. Every ornament is drawn in inline SVG. The collection’s illustrations are procedural artwork, not project screenshots.

                    The visitor’s guestbook

                    The Guestbook

                    Write a note. It travels by email to Shaurya.

                    This site has no server, so there is no public wall of messages and nothing is stored. Your email app opens with the letter ready to send. A hidden trap field turns away simple spam bots, and Shaurya reads every message himself.
